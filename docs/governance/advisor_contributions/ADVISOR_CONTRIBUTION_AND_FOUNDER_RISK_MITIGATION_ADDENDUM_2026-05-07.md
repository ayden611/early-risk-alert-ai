# Advisor Contribution & Founder-Risk Mitigation Addendum — 2026-05-07

## Purpose

This addendum corrects and clarifies how Early Risk Alert AI should describe team/advisor support and founder-related risk in investor, hospital, pilot, governance, and valuation materials.

The company should not be described as having “no team,” “no advisor support,” “no clinical input,” or an “unsupported solo founder” posture.

The accurate framing is:

> Early Risk Alert AI is founder-led with documented clinical and technical advisory support. The remaining risk is founder-led operational dependency and continuity risk, not absence of team or absence of clinical/technical input.

## Corrected Risk Classification

### Incorrect / Incomplete Framing

Do not use these as standalone statements:

- Single founder risk with no team
- Unsupported solo founder
- No clinical advisor
- No clinical input
- No technical/security advisor
- No advisor-informed product changes
- Founder has no external support

### Correct Framing

Use this language instead:

> Early Risk Alert AI is founder-led with active clinical and technical advisory support. Clinical Advisor Andrene Louison, RN has provided targeted clinical guidance that informed Command Center updates, including respiratory-rate context, temperature context, and clinical review/workflow framing. Technical Infrastructure & Security Advisor Uche Anosike is also listed in platform governance materials and supports the platform’s technical/security credibility. Remaining risk is founder-led execution and operational continuity, because core development, validation, governance documentation, deployment, and outreach remain primarily founder-driven.

## What These Contributions Reduce

The documented advisor support reduces:

- Clinical credibility risk
- Product-context risk
- Workflow-alignment risk
- Vital-sign interpretation risk
- Technical/security credibility risk
- Advisor/reference credibility gap
- Perception that the platform was developed without clinical or technical input

## What Risk Still Remains

The remaining risk should be described narrowly as:

> Founder-led operational dependency risk.

This means that Milton Munroe remains the primary operator for many core functions, including:

- Product development
- Validation work
- Governance documentation
- Evidence lock maintenance
- Website wording updates
- Deployment coordination
- Pilot packet preparation
- Investor / hospital outreach

This is a normal early-stage startup risk, but it should not be confused with lack of advisor support.

## Important Boundary

Advisor input is meaningful and should be credited, but it should not be described as:

- Clinical validation
- Prospective validation
- FDA clearance
- FDA approval
- Diagnosis
- Treatment direction
- Independent escalation
- Outcome improvement proof

The platform remains decision-support only and does not diagnose, direct treatment, replace clinician judgment, or independently trigger escalation.
