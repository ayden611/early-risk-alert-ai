# Incident Response Tabletop — 2026-05-07

## Scenario

A hospital reviewer reports that a public webpage appears to overstate validation claims.

## Objective

Show that Early Risk Alert AI can identify, contain, correct, and document claims-control issues.

## Response Steps

1. Confirm the exact page and wording.
2. Compare the wording against the Claims Control Lock.
3. If unsafe, remove or replace the wording immediately.
4. Document the issue in the issue/change log.
5. Capture before/after screenshots.
6. Confirm the corrected live page.
7. Notify relevant reviewer/advisor if needed.

## Severity

- Severity: Medium
- Reason: Public claim risk, but no patient-care action is triggered.

## Decision-Support Guardrail

Early Risk Alert AI does not diagnose, direct treatment, replace clinician judgment, or independently trigger escalation.

## Evidence To Save

- Screenshot of issue
- Screenshot of correction
- Date/time of correction
- Commit or Wix publish record
- Written note of root cause
