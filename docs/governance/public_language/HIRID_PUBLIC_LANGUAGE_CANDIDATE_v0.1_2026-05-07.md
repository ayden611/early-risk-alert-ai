## Purpose

This document proposes conservative public-language options for describing HiRID status without claiming HiRID validation, prospective validation, final performance, FPR, detection rate, lead time, diagnosis, treatment direction, or independent escalation.

This is a governance-controlled draft only. It should not be copied to the live website, investor materials, public pages, pitch decks, grant materials, or hospital packets until approved.

## Current Approved Public Wording

Use this wording on live/public materials right now:

> HiRID access approved; HiRID retrospective aggregate validation pending local evaluation.

## Why This Draft Exists

Early Risk Alert AI has prepared local/private HiRID evaluation workflows and methodology controls. However, HiRID remains private/local and methodologically separate from MIMIC-IV and eICU.

Current HiRID-related materials do not support a public claim of clinical validation, prospective validation, final HiRID performance, time-window FPR, detection rate, lead time, diagnosis, treatment direction, outcome improvement, or independent escalation.

## Phrases Not Approved for Public Use

Do not use these phrases in live/public materials:

- HiRID validated
- three-dataset validation completed
- clinically validated
- prospective validation
- final HiRID performance
- HiRID FPR
- HiRID detection rate
- HiRID lead time
- predicts deterioration
- prevents adverse events
- diagnosis
- treatment direction
- independent escalation
- automatic escalation
- replaces clinician judgment
- FDA cleared
- FDA approved
- FDA exempt

## Safe Replacement Patterns

| Instead of saying | Say |
|---|---|
| HiRID validated | HiRID access approved; HiRID retrospective aggregate validation pending local evaluation |
| Three-dataset validation completed | MIMIC-IV and eICU retrospective aggregate evidence are public-safe; HiRID remains pending local evaluation |
| Prospective validation | Future site-approved prospective or shadow-mode evaluation would require legal/privacy/security and IRB/QI review |
| Final HiRID performance | HiRID-related materials remain pending controlled local review |
| HiRID FPR / detection / lead time | HiRID outcome-proxy review remains private/local and is not equivalent to event-window FPR, detection, or lead-time evidence |
