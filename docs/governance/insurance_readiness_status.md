# Insurance Readiness Status

Early Risk Alert AI, LLC maintains active Technology Errors & Omissions / Cyber Liability coverage as part of its pilot-readiness governance controls.

Insurance readiness does not alter the platform boundary.

No live PHI flows or live hospital integrations are currently active.

## Regulatory Boundary
Active insurance coverage does not constitute FDA clearance, FDA approval, hospital authorization, prospective clinical validation, or proof of patient-outcome improvement.
