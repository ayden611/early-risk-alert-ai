Intended use

Early Risk Alert AI is an HCP-facing decision-support and workflow-support platform designed to help authorized healthcare professionals identify patients who may warrant further clinical evaluation, support patient prioritization, and improve command-center operational awareness.

Decision support only. Not intended to diagnose, direct treatment, replace clinician judgment, or independently trigger escalation.

Evidence status

Canonical internal evidence accepted · Public numerical release on hold

The August canonical MIMIC-IV and eICU reverification records state ACCEPTED_CANONICAL_INTERNAL_EVIDENCE. Their public-release status remains HOLD. This page presents a qualitative summary and does not publish performance figures or grant numerical public-release clearance.

Public numerical evidence remains withheld pending the dated release-clearance decision required by the existing claim-content adjudication. Rebuilding this page does not itself change that status.

Evidence tracks and their limits

MIMIC-IV strict clinical-event full cohort
Canonical retrospective internal evidence. The immutable August reverification record is the authority for this track; public numerical release remains on hold.
eICU harmonized clinical-event full cohort
Canonical retrospective internal evidence under a separate August reverification record and public-release hold. Its event definition is not interchangeable with the MIMIC-IV definition.
Historical eICU outcome-proxy
Mortality/discharge-derived endpoint context, preserved as historical reference. It is not direct deterioration-event validation and must not be combined or averaged with harmonized clinical-event results.
Historical harmonized full-cohort and subcohort references
Earlier full-cohort results are retired as canonical product evidence. The B/C subcohort results remain historical internal robustness checkpoints; their comparability with the adopted canonical scorer has not been established.

No cross-dataset equivalence, combined detection result, or current product-performance claim follows from these different evidence tracks.

Limits of the evidence

Retrospective aggregate evaluation does not establish prospective clinical validation, diagnostic performance, patient-level probability, treatment benefit, measured clinician-burden reduction, or financial return. Dataset-specific event definitions and historical scoring paths limit comparisons.

Restricted source data and row-level derived files are outside this public summary. No patient records or individualized clinical recommendations are presented.

Authority and public-use status

The May evidence manifest authenticates membership and byte identity at its historical baseline. It does not confer current canonical status or clear the later August results for public release. The August run-specific aggregates and acceptance records are separate authorities.

Historical evidence files remain preserved. Current reader-facing wording follows the claim-content adjudication ERA-CLAIM-ADJ-2026-08-10-C1-R2, including the distinction between accepted internal evidence and public-release clearance.
